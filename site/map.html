🗺️ The Map

This is the entire build, in order, on one page. The route is real work, and order is the method. You don't have to remember where you are, your AI does (ask it "where are we on how.nixfred.com?"). But when a session ends, or you come back days later, or you just get lost, this is the page you return to.

Every stop has a 📋 copy command. Click it, paste it into your AI, and it drops straight back into that step, checks the work before it, and keeps going. You're the one who hits enter; the AI does the reading.

How to use this map
  1. Find the last step you finished and got to 100%.
  2. The next stop down is where you go.
  3. Open its 📋 Drop my AI into this step, copy it, paste it to your AI. That's it.
